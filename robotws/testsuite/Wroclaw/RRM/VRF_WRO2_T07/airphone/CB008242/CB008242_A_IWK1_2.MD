## 1. DESCRIPTION 

- This test scenario explaining how to perform the gNB behaviour when C-plane detects CU overload and overload are switched off. 
The gNB sets the C-plane gNB-CU overload trigger as ACTIVE in all cells with automatic UAC configured and starts broadcast 
max automatic UAC configuration in SIB1 for them. Next test of the gNB behaviour when C-plane transits from Overload ON to Overload OFF 
and declares the gNB overload condition is OFF for resumption of normal services. The gNB sets the C-plane gNB-CU overload 
trigger as INACTIVE in all cells and gradually downgrades UAC settings to loosen cell access till auto UAC configuration 
is no longer broadcasted in SIB1. gNB broadcasts static UAC configuration in SIB1 if configured - otherwise gNB does not 
broadcast uac-BarringForCommon IE in SIB1. Also checks proper incrementation of `TIME_AUTO_UAC_OVL`, `TIME_AUTO_UAC` and `TIME_AUTO_UAC_MAX` counters.

### 2. Test Steps

#### Step 1: Prepare configuration.

- Description: Perform Overload in CU:
  - Change `rdOverloadHighThreshold` to 0 by `set_overload_high_threshold` on all CU_CPUE instances, overload should be ON by CU.
- Expected:
  - gNB-CU send to gNB-DU `F1AP: NETWORK ACCESS RATE REDUCTION` <- message is visible in tcpdump
  - gNB-DU starts `startUacBarringTimerOverload` <- SIB1 is not changed before expiration of `startUacBarringTimerOverload`

#### Step 2: Wait for max time of startUacBarringTimerOverload from all cells.

- Description: gNB should change SIB1 after `startUacBarringTimerOverload` cells #1, #2 and #3.
- Expected:
  - Cells #1, #2 and #3 with overload automatic UAC trigger configured broadcast the maximum configuration of automatic UAC parameters in SIB1 (check SIB1s of cells: gNB2:1, gNB2:2, gNB2:3 in Egate and/or Uu).
  - Please check `EFaultId_AutomaticUnifiedAccessControlBarringAl` is raised only on correct cell (ONLY on Cells #1 and #3).
  - Please check time between overload ON and SIB1s changes is corresponding to `startUacBarringTimerOverload` from proper cells.
  - Please check uac-BarringInfo IEs is the same as in MAX/Th4 in NRUAC_ AUTO_PROFILE.
![](https://nokia.sharepoint.com/sites/I_EXT_5G_ET_TEAM4_GMS/Shared%20Documents/New%20Features/CB008242/TestCases_Draft_postCP2/OVL_IWK1.png)
 
#### Step 3: Perform CU overload OFF.

- Description: Change rdOverloadHighThreshold to 20 (or other value) by set_overload_high_threshold on all CU_CPUE instances, overload should be OFF by CU.
- Expected:
  - gNB-CU stops send to gNB-DU `F1AP: NETWORK ACCESS RATE REDUCTION` message.

#### Step 4: Auto UAC Downgrading.

- Description: gNB-DU starts `stopUacBarringTimerOverload` and `stopUacBarringTimerOverload` expires. Then `uacUpdateTimer` expired. DU perform 1st SIB1 UAC downgrading [please see USCB008242-B#B2 too]
- Expected:
  - When `uacUpdateTimer` expires the gNB-DU executes SIB1 UAC downgrade procedure with Th3 settings. `uacUpdateTimer` is started.
  - When `uacUpdateTimer` expires the gNB-DU executes SIB1 UAC downgrade procedure with Th2 settings. `uacUpdateTimer` is started. 
  - When `uacUpdateTimer` expires the gNB-DU executes SIB1 UAC downgrade procedure with Th1 settings. `uacUpdateTimer` is started. 
  - When `uacUpdateTimer` expires and none of load triggers is ACTIVE the gNB starts to broadcast SIB1 with static UAC/ no UAC settings. Static UAC is visible in SIB1 of cells: #1 and #2. NO UAC is visible in SIB1 of cells #3 and #4.
  - If fault `EFaultId_AutomaticUnifiedAccessControlBarringAl` has been raised for the cell, gNB-DU cancels the fault. Faults should be cancelled on cells #1 and #3. 

#### Step 5: Check downgrading mechanism.

- Description: `stopUacBarringTimerOverload` and `uacUpdateTimer` are internal gNB timers but working results should be visible in Egate and tcpdumps on Uu interface.
- Expected:
  - Please check time between:
    -  Overload OFF and 1st autoUAC downgrade in SIB1. Time must be greater or equal to `stopUacBarringTimerOverload`
    -  1st and 2nd SIB1 change should be equal to `uacUpdateTimer`
    -  2nd and 3rd SIB1 change should be equal to `uacUpdateTimer`
    -  3rd and no autoUAC SIB1 change should be equal to `uacUpdateTimer`
  - Please check SIB1s UACs:
    -  Auto UAC IEs in SIB1 in MAX phase are visible and correct to MAX on cells #1 #2 #3, it is not visible in cell #4.
    -  Auto UAC IEs in SIB1 in 3 phase are visible and correct on cells #1 #2 #3, it is not visible in cell #4.
    -  Auto UAC IEs in SIB1 in 2 phase are visible and correct on cells #1 #2 #3, it is not visible in cell #4.
    -  Auto UAC IEs in SIB1 in 1 phase are visible and correct on cells #1 #2 #3, it is not visible in cell #4.

![](https://nokia.sharepoint.com/sites/I_EXT_5G_ET_TEAM4_GMS/Shared%20Documents/New%20Features/CB008242/TestCases_Draft_postCP2/OVL_IWK2.png)

#### Step 6: Check No Alarm EFaultId_AutomaticUnifiedAccessControlBarringAl On Cells.

- No alarm visible on all cells

#### Step 7 : Check that counters was decremented by gNB

- The `TIME_AUTO_UAC_OVL`, `TIME_AUTO_UAC` and `TIME_AUTO_UAC_MAX` counters was decremented by gNB.

### 3. Postconditions

- The all cells does NOT broadcast automatic UAC parameters in SIB1.
- The cells 1 and 2 does broadcast static UAC parameters in SIB1.
- The cells 3 and 4 does broadcast NO UAC parameters in SIB1.
- No alarm visible.
- All cells are on air.
- The `TIME_AUTO_UAC_OVL`, `TIME_AUTO_UAC` and `TIME_AUTO_UAC_MAX` counters was decremented by gNB.
